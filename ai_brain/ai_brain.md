# AI Brain — Combined Knowledge Base

> This is the shared, system-wide knowledge base aggregated across all agents (Analyst, Optimizer, Debugger, and any future agents). It is the single source of truth that all agents should consult before starting work and update after completing it.

## Purpose
- Prevent knowledge silos between agents.
- Surface cross-agent patterns that no single agent would notice alone.
- Serve as the long-term "collective memory" of the AI Arena system.

## Structure
- `ai_brain.md` (this file) — index and high-level summary.
- `problem_solutions.md` — catalog of issues encountered and their fixes.
- `system_insights.md` — collective learnings, patterns, and best practices.

## How to Use
1. Before starting a task, agents should check `problem_solutions.md` for known issues and `system_insights.md` for relevant patterns.
2. After completing a task, agents should promote any generalizable learning here (not agent-specific memory).
3. Keep entries concise, dated, and attributed to the agent/task that produced them.
